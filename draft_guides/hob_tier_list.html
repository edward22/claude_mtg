Draft guide · limited environment notes

HOB — The Hobbit

A first read on all 188 nonbasic cards in the set, evaluated for a standard draft pod. Five tribes carry the format — Dwarves (Storied/Equipment, R/W), Elves (Landfall, G/U), Goblins & Orcs (Amass, B/R), Wolves (Ferocious, B/G), and Lake-town's Humans (Recruit, W/U) — with Halflings and Bears running as smaller color-pair threads through black-blue and green. Tiers below reflect raw card power and how often a card is good regardless of what else you've drafted; the top two bands get full notes, everything else is placed and grouped by color for a quick scan during a pick.

Set mechanics, quickly

Amass Goblins N
— put N +1/+1 counters on an Army you control (making/growing a 0/0 Goblin token).
Storied
— control 3+ artifacts/legendaries/Sagas for a permanent bonus.
Recruit
— loot; discarding a nonland makes a 1/1 Human Soldier.
Ferocious
— bonus while you control a creature with power 4+.
Landfall
— trigger whenever a land you control enters.
Hone counter
— on Equipment, grants +1/+0 to the creature it's on.
Adventure
— cast the instant/sorcery half, then cast the creature later from exile.
